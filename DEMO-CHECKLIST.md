# AgentX Learn presentation checklist

Use the fixed, reviewed **Expense procedure readiness — fictional pilot** and its
built-in receipt-policy revision. Do not generate a new course during the timed
presentation. This avoids the unverified live course-generation path and keeps the
presenter focused on evidence, trainer control and the changed procedure.

The default dependable rehearsal is **offline simulation**. An optional live
coach/chat segment requires a configured gateway and agreed model usage. The
earlier one-use validation authorization is not permission to repeat paid calls.
OpenClaw has not been live-verified; do not describe the current app as a verified
OpenClaw deployment. A planned next-day demonstration is not a confirmed organizer
deadline or presentation slot.

## On-stage sequence

The optional three-minute script assumes steps 1–7 above are complete. Rehearse the
timing; do not promise the full unprepared journey fits that slot.

| Order | Action and narration | Observable acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Show the mode banner. Say: “The original learner evidence was prepared in rehearsal; this run is an offline simulation.” For an explicitly arranged live run, name the actual gateway and show its live label. | The audience can tell which actions are prepared and whether a model is involved. |
| 2 | As `learner`, show the original receipt gap and its saved **Why this next step?** decision; expand **Earlier decisions** if needed. | The source section and recorded lesson outcome are visible. Do not claim the saved selection just occurred on stage. |
| 3 | Switch to `trainer`, show the reviewed revision and mapping, check the review confirmation, then click **Approve version and assign refreshers**. | The revision becomes activated and the previous version remains in history. Do not skip or pretend to have performed content review. |
| 4 | Return as the same `learner`; choose the updated course. | Three unchanged objectives display accepted earlier evidence; receipt evidence requires fresh practice. Readiness has not yet been restored. |
| 5 | Click **Coach me through the next step**, review the changed receipt lesson, then complete a fresh case using the displayed source. | Lesson review alone does not award readiness. A correct fresh case restores readiness for version two; the decision card accurately labels offline or live selection. |
| 6 | As `trainer`, open **Progress → View evidence**. | The current result, carried evidence and changed objective are inspectable. Superseded history remains available and does not inflate current dashboard totals. |

Explain that carrying three of four objective records is **not** evidence of 75%
time savings. The model selects eligible activities in live mode; the backend
validates eligibility and scores answers. Trainer approval controls policy changes.

## Optional Q&A, outside the timed sequence

- Ask “What should I do if my receipt is missing?” in **Ask your assistant** and
  expand the citation. Before revision, expect the original receipt rule; after
  activation, use the newly approved source. Do not judge a version-two answer
  against the withdrawn version-one text.
- Ask “What is the capital of France?” Expect insufficient approved evidence and
  referral to the trainer, not an invented policy answer.
- Show the fixed course, source approval and review controls. Do not click
  **Create course draft** merely to fill time: live course generation has not passed
  its latest validation, and the paid recheck remains unapproved.
- A saved review request can demonstrate trainer guidance. Guidance must not alter
  the learner's score or award readiness.

## Explicit offline fallback

If the live gateway errors, say what failed and stop issuing paid retries. The app
does not silently fall back. Use a separately prepared **Offline simulation**
rehearsal, or create one through **trainer → Progress → Start fresh offline demo**.
A fresh rehearsal starts without learner progress; it does not inherit the failed
live run's evidence. Switch browser profiles when preserving two prepared runs.

If the application itself cannot be demonstrated, open
[backup-demo.gif](backup-demo.gif) and identify it as a **silent illustrated
reconstruction**, not a screen recording, live model call or substitute for the
organizer's required video. [REHEARSAL.md](REHEARSAL.md) contains the historical
observation; it does not prove the current checkout was just exercised.
