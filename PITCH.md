# AgentX Learn: prove the changed step

## Opening — 25 seconds

“Employees can finish a course and still miss a critical procedure. And when that
procedure changes, a completion badge tells us very little about what they know now.
AgentX Learn connects approved guidance, targeted practice and version-specific
evidence of readiness.”

## Show the agent — 45 seconds

“Our fictional learner missed receipt evidence. The agent reads that saved result
and selects an eligible approved lesson. Here is the decision record: the gap,
the source section and the chosen activity. Reading the lesson does not award a
pass. The learner must apply the rule in a different case. The backend scores that
answer and requires every critical objective.”

## Show the update — 65 seconds

“Now the receipt procedure changes: when a supplier cannot replace a receipt, the
employee must obtain a written Finance exception. The trainer reviews the new
source and cases and approves the refresh mapping. Three unchanged objectives keep
their demonstrated evidence. Only the changed receipt objective needs fresh practice.
The old version and original attempts remain in history.”

## Show the result and close — 45 seconds

“The learner passes a new receipt case and regains readiness for version two.
The trainer can inspect readiness, critical gaps, carried evidence and outstanding
refreshers. This goes beyond answering policy questions: it closes the loop from
an observed gap to fresh evidence, and reopens only the affected part when guidance
changes. Our prototype demonstrates that workflow; a real pilot would next measure
retention, trainer workload and unnecessary repeated training.”

## Demonstration order

For a three-minute slot, prepare completed original-policy evidence and a reviewed
but unactivated revision beforehand. Disclose that these are saved rehearsal results.
Show the earlier decision record, activate the reviewed revision, then perform the
updated lesson and fresh case live. Do not try to answer all diagnostic and original
assessment questions during the pitch. Keep the full journey available for Q&A.

## Questions judges may ask

- **What is agentic?** The model selects an eligible activity using saved learner
  evidence and chooses read tools for grounded policy questions. The application
  revalidates its choice before issuance.
- **Does AI decide who passes?** No. Scoring and critical-objective requirements are
  deterministic. Trainer guidance does not award a pass.
- **Does AI identify policy changes?** This prototype uses deterministic comparison
  and explicit trainer approval. Semantic impact analysis is future work.
- **Did you save 75% of training time?** We carried three of four objective records
  in this fictional example. We have not measured time savings or learning outcomes.
- **How reliable is the model?** Three consecutive gateway smoke runs passed after
  bounded JSON recovery. That is integration evidence, not a reliability benchmark.
- **What if the network fails?** Use the labeled offline replay or a separately
  labeled offline rehearsal. Never describe either as a live model call.
